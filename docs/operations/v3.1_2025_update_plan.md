# v3.1 / 2025 Data Update Plan

**Status:** draft
**Target release:** Q2 2026 (v3.1 addendum) · Q3 2026 (v4.0 full 2025)
**Current shipped version:** v3.0.3 (1990–2024, released January 2026)

---

## Goal

Extend the Deflation Index with measured 2025 data where authoritative sources have published, and defer sectors where 2025 primary data will not be available until summer 2026. Avoid mixing measured and projected values inside the weighted index.

## Two-release strategy

- **v3.1 — "2025 early read"** (target: Q2 2026). Ships a 2025 appendix to the existing v3.0.3 index. The weighted headline number remains 1990–2024. A separate 2025 panel reports per-sector values that have been measured, with clearly labeled gaps.
- **v4.0 — "Through 2025"** (target: Q3 2026). Full recalculation once IRENA's *Renewable Power Generation Costs in 2025* and NREL ATB 2025 publish. Headline DI extends to 2025 with all four sectors populated from measured sources.

## Open decisions for Israel

1. **CPI gap treatment.** Interpolation is the pragmatic default, but we could also publish 2025 CPI as "Sep 2025 → Dec 2025 only" and explicitly skip the missing months in the headline. Pick one.
2. **v3.1 vs. waiting for v4.0.** If the signal value of a 2025 early-read panel is low, we could skip v3.1 entirely and go straight to v4.0 in summer 2026. Cleaner, but means eight more months of v3.0.3 as the public face.
3. **Battery sector disaggregation.** BNEF now publishes separate BEV ($99) and stationary ($70) pack prices. We can keep the single average or break them out — the latter is more informative but requires weighting within the sector.
4. **Paywalled sources.** BNEF battery data is behind a paywall but the headline number is public. For v4.0 consider whether we want a subscription for deeper coverage or stick with press-release figures.
