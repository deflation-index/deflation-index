## v3.1.1 — April 19, 2026

Incremental refresh on top of v3.1.0. What changed:

- Direct FRED CSV download succeeded (M2SL and CPIAUCSL), replacing the earlier press-release-only figures with the full 2025 monthly time series.
- `data/constants.json` `supplemental_2025` block rewritten to include the 12 monthly M2 and CPI values plus a 2025 annual average for each.
- `data/api/m2_data.json` extended with a 2025 annual entry (`m2_billions: 21967.7`, `index_value: 670.40`, `yoy_change_pct: 3.13`) rebased to the existing 1990 anchor ($3,276.8B) to preserve continuity with the 1990–2024 series.
- CPI Oct 2025 linearly interpolated between Sep (324.245) and Nov (325.063) → 324.654. Only Oct was missing; Nov and Dec 2025 were both released by BLS in a late-December 2025 update.
- `note_on_rebasing` added to `supplemental_2025.m2` explaining the 1.7% discrepancy between FRED-live 1990 ($3,222.2B) and the existing series 1990 ($3,276.8B) — a routine seasonal-factor revision to be reconciled in the v4.0 full rebase.
- Dec 2025 M2 headline updated on `index.html` from the press-release rounded $22.37T to the live FRED-retrieved $22.39T ($22,386.9B). YoY framed as the +3.95% 2024→2025 annual-average change for consistency with `constants.json`.

Nothing else changed. The weighted DI, sector weights, and all 1990–2024 source figures remain intact.

## Summary

v3.1 is an *additive* release. The weighted Deflation Index 1990–2024 is unchanged. What is new:

1. A 2025 early-read panel on the landing page showing measured 2025 inputs for M2, CPI, lithium-ion battery pack pricing, and AI compute price-performance.
2. A `supplemental_2025` block in `data/constants.json` holding the raw 2025 headline figures with source provenance and retrieval dates.
3. Updated `SOURCES.md` (bumped to v3.1.0) flagging which sections updated and which still cite 2024 sources.
4. Version stamp bump in the site footer from `v3.0.3 · data reflects year-end 2024` to `v3.1 · weighted DI through 2024 · 2025 early read`.

What is deliberately *not* changed:

- The 1990–2024 annual data tables in `data/constants.json` (`m2_money_supply.annual_data`, `cpi`, `deflation_index`) are untouched. The 2024 endpoint remains `DI = 3.74`, `M2 = $21,300B (index 650.20)`, `CPI = 254.65`.
- The weighted sector workbooks (`data/excel/master_deflation_index_v3.0.3.xlsx` and the EQUAL variant) are not rebuilt. They are the canonical 1990–2024 product until v4.0.
- Sector weights are unchanged (Computing 29.4%, Communications 23.5%, Energy 29.4%, Transportation 17.6%).

## Provenance note — FRED direct access

At release time, the Cowork runtime used to build v3.1 did not have direct network egress to `fred.stlouisfed.org`, even after allowlist configuration. Values above were confirmed from public press releases, the Federal Reserve's H.6 release page, and BLS news releases rather than from a raw FRED CSV fetch in the same session. The numbers are authoritative headline figures published by the originating agencies; exact monthly time series extension for `data/m2_data.json` is deferred to v3.1.1 (or v4.0) once direct FRED CSV retrieval is available. When that happens:

- `data/m2_data.json` will be extended with 2025 monthly M2 values.
- `constants.json` will receive a 2025 annual-average entry inside `m2_money_supply.annual_data`.
- An equivalent CPI 2025 annual block will be added, with the Oct–Nov 2025 interpolation method documented inline.

This release deliberately publishes only the confirmed Dec-endpoint and YoY figures rather than imprecise annual averages. The panel on the landing page labels each figure with its scope (Dec 2025, 12-month, etc.) to avoid conflating an early-read with a full-year measurement.

## CPI Oct/Nov 2025 treatment — decision

Per the v3.1 update plan, the two missing CPI months are handled by **linear interpolation between Sep 2025 and Dec 2025** when annual averages are eventually computed. The Dec 2025 year-over-year figure (+2.7%) is published as a point estimate and carries no interpolation.

## Blockers for v4.0 (full 2025 weighted index)

v4.0 will rebuild the weighted DI with 2025 endpoints for all four sectors. It is gated on:

| Source | Expected | Impact on v4.0 |
|---|---|---|
| IRENA *Renewable Power Generation Costs in 2025* | ~July 2026 | Authoritative 2025 LCOE for solar, onshore/offshore wind → Energy sector |
| NREL ATB 2025 | Late spring 2026 | Reference capital costs for energy technologies → Energy sector |
| FCC Internet Access Services (2025 H1 or later) | Late 2026 / early 2027 | Broadband subscriptions and speeds → Communications sector |
| DOE Transportation Energy Data Book Ed. 41 | Still delayed | Edition 40 (2022 data) remains current; pre-existing gap from v3.0.3 |

CTIA, IEA, and SEC EDGAR 10-K filings may fill some intermediate gaps before v4.0.
