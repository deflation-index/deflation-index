Then: A solar kilowatt-hour cost 28 cents in 2010 (in 2010 dollars).

Now: 4.4 cents — and the decline has plateaued.

IRENA measures an 89% real-terms decline since 2010; in nominal dollars — the basis every sector here uses — it is 84%. And honestly: the curve has flattened, and in nominal terms solar LCOE has risen since 2023. We report the plateau too.
