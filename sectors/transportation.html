Then: An EV battery pack was $1,160/kWh in 2010.

Now: $108 — a ten-fold drop in fifteen years.

The battery is the car. Packs plateaued near $140 from 2020 to 2023, then resumed falling: $115 in 2024, $108 in 2025 ($99 BEV-specific). Below $100, EVs undercut gas cars on sticker price for good.
